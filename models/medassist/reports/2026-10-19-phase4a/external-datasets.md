## Source-specific findings and blockers

**DDXPlus.** [Official author documentation](https://github.com/mila-iqia/ddxplus) and [English v2 metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22687585/versions/2) verify public CC BY 4.0 synthetic data. Age/sex, initial evidence, question text and binary/categorical/multichoice evidence support staged questioning. Preserve published partitions; reveal an answer only when asked. Pathology, full evidence list, differential and condition severity are hidden oracle/reference fields, never patient-visible inputs. Proprietary generation rules limit independence and realism. No synthetic diagnosis severity becomes NHAMCS or home-triage truth. No raw files were acquired.

**SYNAPSE.** [Publisher v4 metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/28547042/versions/4), DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.28547042.v4, reports 130,637 synthetic profiles and CC BY 4.0; direct file URL is recorded in the manifest. Symptoms, gender, binned age and duration precede the recommendation conceptually, but expert-assessed `Severity` is not automatically available to a home user and could leak the target. Final labels are OTC Drug versus Doctor Consultation, without emergency timing, outcomes or representative Indian patient sampling. No prescribing use or transfer of these labels is proposed.

**CARE-Bench.** The [author release card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ningkko/CARE-bench/main/README.md) lists 439 public cases/925 prefixes and 61 controlled-access cases/134 input-only prefixes. Dataset metadata says CC BY-NC 4.0; code licence remains unspecified without a LICENSE file. Original-source terms still govern underlying material. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03731v1) describes LLM-assisted reconstruction, screening by a public-health PhD candidate and clinical questions reviewed by a medical trainee. This is not an independent practising-clinician panel or a real prospective cohort. Labels include inferred information-need logic; urgent combines several care intensities. Public prefixes can support software experiments after review; controlled-access data are excluded. India/all-age representativeness, label reliability and contamination remain unresolved. Do not import its runner or raw sources automatically.

**PMR-Bench.** The [author paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13178v1) distinguishes public Reddit, public synthetic-message/real-EHR combinations and proprietary PMR-Real. The [public collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/PortalPal-AI/medical-triage-as-pairwise-ranking) links the subsets; [Reddit test metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PortalPal-AI/PMR-Reddit-Test-Pairs) declares CC BY-NC 4.0. Pairwise higher-urgency preferences are not an absolute safe disposition. Source comments and preference labels must remain evaluator-only; EHR context may contain facts unavailable at the conversation time. Public posts have sensitive-data/reuse concerns; public availability is not consent. Synthetic-EHR upstream terms need separate verification. Proprietary real clinical messages are excluded. No Indian or all-age validation claim follows.

**MedAsk / Semigran.** [Author triage documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medaks/medask-benchmarks/master/triage_bench/README.md) identifies 45 Semigran vignettes and three urgency categories. [Repository MIT licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/medaks/medask-benchmarks/master/LICENSE) covers software; it cannot establish third-party vignette rights. The [original BMJ study](https://www.bmj.com/content/351/bmj.h3480) returned HTTP 403 in this research tool, so original supplement terms were not independently confirmed. Before reuse obtain the original source, exact licence, attribution and provenance. These static clinician-style vignettes lack natural adaptive-dialogue ground truth and are widely exposed, increasing contamination risk. Reference diagnoses must not reach the evaluated system. Small constructed samples cannot measure prevalence or rare-case safety in India.

**MIMIC demo.** [PhysioNet v2.2](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ed-demo/2.2/) confirms anyone may access under ODbL 1.0. It is a small real deidentified US ED subset; its source triage schema is hospital-based. Attribution and applicable database share-alike terms matter. Prior decision remains smoke tests only, not training/benchmark validation. Full credentialed MIMIC data remain excluded; no acquisition occurred.

**NHAMCS 2021.** [CDC documentation index](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhamcs/documentation/index.html) and [2021 codebook](https://ftp.cdc.gov/pub/Health_Statistics/NCHS/Dataset_Documentation/NHAMCS/doc21-ed-508.pdf) establish a public prior-year ED resource. Statistical/public-use restrictions apply; no identification, linkage or blanket licence to third-party drug terminology is inferred. Audit IMMEDR coding, demographics, units, missingness, survey design and timing anew before acquisition/evaluation. It is another survey wave from the same US system, potentially overlapping institutions, **not** an independently adjudicated home cohort. No 2021 raw checksum, cross-wave patient independence or eligible support has been verified; record them before any future frozen study.

**India-specific evidence.** [Noora Health's study](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09356v1) reports 769 real queries, including 251 emergencies and 177 English queries, from maternal/newborn-oriented care. Two in-house clinicians annotated them under internal protocols. The paper describes government-partnership restrictions; no public patient-data licence/download was identified. Article access does not confer data rights. Use methodology only, not its rules as approved APIP rules. [BODHI's author repository](https://github.com/eka-care/BODHI) offers CC BY-NC 4.0 symptom/condition/speciality knowledge with node urgency annotations. It is an ontology, not independently labelled patient trajectories; India-developed does not imply a representative Indian cohort. Review source evidence, vocabulary/terminology terms and clinical applicability before any retrieval use. Neither closes the external-validation gap.

## Acquisition gates for any future task

Pin a version/commit, source document and metadata digest, actual file checksum/size, licence/attribution, provenance and permitted purpose before ingesting. For sensitive public text, document ethical reuse and disclosure risk; do not republish case text. Set retrospective/synthetic/reconstructed tags at source, keep ground truth away from prediction inputs, and prevent case/source-family overlap. Recheck changing pages and upstream terms. This phase authorizes neither restricted downloads nor treatment recommendations nor mixing any of these labels into v1.
