# Independent MedAssist validation study — draft for clinical review

**Status: research design only; no clinical rules, care time limits or release thresholds approved.** Phase 3 ED outputs are excluded from patient-facing decisions. This document does not authorize participant recruitment, patient-data acquisition, a clinical pilot, new inference or training. English only, free local tooling where feasible, no mandatory paid service.

## Question, population and reference standard

Evaluate whether a future system gathers relevant information, recognizes insufficient information and communicates an appropriate **current next action** for a person describing symptoms at home. Record urgency/time-to-care separately from care setting. Primary care, an appropriate specialist, emergency services and a reviewed non-prescriptive information response are distinct possibilities; seeking more information and unable-to-assess are explicit outputs, not ordinal severity classes. Do not convert IMMEDR or synthetic pathology severity into any of these outcomes.

All-age intent requires explicit neonatal/infant, child, adolescent, adult and older-adult strata plus caregiver, pregnancy/postpartum and relevant chronic-condition contexts. Clinical reviewers must decide appropriate age-band definitions and scope. Unsupported populations remain explicitly unevaluated, not pooled into an all-age pass. Indian urban/rural access, health literacy, local care pathways and facility capability need review; US ED labels or an Indian-language dataset alone cannot establish transportability.

Proposed reference panel: two independently rating appropriately qualified clinicians, with primary-care/emergency expertise and paediatric/obstetric input for those cases; a third qualified adjudicator resolves disagreements. Reviewers must not see model outputs or baseline scores while labelling. Record qualifications, conflicts, agreed scope, rubric/evidence version and disagreements before adjudication. Mahesh can coordinate and provide pharmacy-domain review within his verified scope; neither his title nor dean consultation supplies unverified clinical qualifications or study approvals. Codex's checks remain AI-assisted engineering/evidence work.

For each case and disclosure prefix record: facts available, explicit absent findings, unanswered/unknown/declined facts, contradictions, allowed clarifying questions, evidence-supported action or acceptable action set, maximum acceptable delay/range where reviewers can justify it, acceptable care settings, warning-sign onset, uncertainty reasons, forbidden reassurance/prescription, and source/rubric provenance. If reviewers cannot support a reference label, retain a disputed/insufficient-evidence status and prespecified analysis handling rather than force certainty. Report raw agreement and agreement statistics before adjudication, not only final consensus.

## Separate data tracks

1. **A — preserved ED baseline.** Phase 4A counts are retrospective descriptions of the opened Phase 3 test. Any later ED research needs a newly frozen protocol and independent data. It cannot count as independent home-task validation.
2. **B — synthetic conversation software research.** DDXPlus uses initial evidence and on-demand answers; the simulator hides full evidence, diagnosis, differential and severity from the system. Judge question relevance, answer extraction, coverage and cost, not clinical urgency. Other synthetic/reconstructed datasets retain their own tags/labels and are reported separately, never pooled with real clinical ground truth.
3. **C — independent clinician-reviewed scenario study.** Following licence/provenance review, prepare staged vignettes and new local-context cases not derived from test error examples or system responses. Published challenge sets can support exploratory testing; they are not guaranteed independent of LLM training. New independently authored hidden cases reduce exposure risk. Record source/case-family duplicates and whether any case was inspected during development.
4. **D — later real clinical external study.** A separate institutionally authorized, ethically reviewed study would need permissioned data, recruitment/consent or appropriate waiver, secure retention/access, clinical responsibility, monitoring and a prespecified analysis. Begin with clinician-only offline/silent evaluation if authorized, without influencing care. This track is blocked here; open-access prototypes do not imply live-patient permission.

## Independence and study freeze

Group all variants, paraphrases and prefixes of one underlying case, source patient and source family together. Where sites/clinicians are available, reserve an external site/time block and record what independence is actually knowable. Create development and untouched evaluation pools **before** tuning. No prompt, question policy, calibration, thresholds, evidence corpus or output parser may be changed after final evaluation opens. Record hashes, split/case-family manifests externally, model/provider/version, prompt, local weights, random seeds and software. No use of the Phase 3 test to tune a new system. A rights issue, missing required class or unresolved implementation failure stops evaluation before test access.

The study freeze must specify sample size by important subgroup and rare warning-sign family, primary endpoint, acceptable harm margins, power/precision justification, missing data/failed outputs, exclusion rules, multiplicity, paired comparisons, stopping rules and who approves acceptance. Values are **pending independent clinical/statistical review**, not borrowed from F2 or engineering guards. Enrich a clearly labelled danger-sign challenge set while separately keeping a representative prevalence-based set; report their metrics separately. For zero observed misses, uncertainty still depends on independent case count and clustering. Repeated prefixes/paraphrases do not create independent patients. No safety percentage is certified by a small clean vignette set.

## Safety endpoints and scoring contract

Use original narrative plus an independently annotated evidence state at each prefix. Evaluate the underlying assessment separately from generated wording; a correct internal action with unsafe text still fails the wording endpoint. Use blinded human coding for clinical meaning. An LLM judge may assist annotation proposals, but cannot be the sole clinical reference or silently adjudicate disagreements. Freeze mapper/parser versions and audit their errors independently. Do not run hosted patient data through a provider without separate permission and governance.

| Endpoint | Prespecified denominator and failure description |
| --- | --- |
| Warning-sign recognition | Reviewer-labelled danger-sign prefixes/cases. Count recognized, missed, delayed escalation and extra questions after sufficient danger information, by sign family and population. Clinical triggers remain proposals until approved. |
| Information sufficiency | Reviewer-labelled insufficient-information prefixes. Count premature assessment/reassurance, appropriate targeted questions, unresolved uncertainty and failure to ask decision-relevant questions. Unknown is not absent. |
| Under-triage | Assessable cases where the action exceeds the reviewer-allowed delay or selects an inadequate care setting. Report urgent missed counts and clinically reviewed severe-harm errors separately; do not infer an ordinal distance from arbitrary labels. |
| Over-triage | Cases with a reviewer-supported lower-intensity action where the response unnecessarily escalates. Report counts, burden and false-positive rate among eligible nonurgent cases; not only among errors. |
| Uncertainty / abstention | All attempted cases, plus urgent cases separately. Count coverage, abstentions, failures and urgent cases withheld. Report selective results **and unconditional urgent detection**; an abstention is not a detected urgent case unless its actual response supplies the approved urgent action. |
| Contradictory answers | Paired case variants with opposite/changed/negated answers, timing conflicts, different caregiver context or incompatible units. Count unacknowledged contradiction, silent overwrite and unsupported inference. Reassessment should use current confirmed facts without erasing source statements. |
| Hallucination / reassurance | All generated responses. Count invented findings/history, unsupported certainty, diagnosis/prescription, fabricated sources, dangerous reassurance and quoted patient text promoted to clinical truth. Score per response and case; report serious events individually to authorized reviewers, never public patient examples. |
| General precautions | Reviewer-approved non-prescriptive content only. Count unsupported or inappropriate precautions, actionable clarity and whether optional wording delays an urgent step. No medication initiation/dose advice is introduced by this study. |
| Care navigation | Cases with reviewer-approved care action. Judge urgency separately from primary-care/specialist/emergency setting, capability, location consent and unavailable-service fallback. A nearby Maps result is not verified capability or “best” care. |

Assess question burden, redundant questions, correction handling, reading comprehension and software failures alongside safety. Predefine language perturbations within English (typos, colloquialisms, negation and incomplete answers), caregiver reports and absent/unreliable home measurements. Counterfactual pairs should change one relevant fact; clinicians approve their expected behavior. Do not substitute automated demographic parity for clinically appropriate subgroup review.

## Uncertainty, reporting and gates

Report support, confusion/error counts and intervals for prespecified endpoints, clustering by case family/source patient as appropriate; choose a defensible method before analysis. Weight only when sampling probabilities support it. Do not use NHAMCS PATWT for new conversation cases, call enriched challenge prevalence population prevalence, or reuse singleton-stratum IID substitutes. Report small-support results as uncertain and withhold unsupported comparative rankings. Account for source dependence and within-case sequential turns; isolate source-family performance and public-set contamination.

Engineering GO requires valid permissions/source versions, leakage-resistant inputs, reproducible harness checks, frozen study and a usable reference rubric. Clinical-study GO additionally needs qualified review, sufficient independent support, approved safety margins and applicable institutional/data arrangements. Deployment requires further independent decisions and operational evidence. Failure of any essential safety endpoint or inability to measure it is not rescued by overall accuracy. **Current decision: GO for offline harness/review preparation only; NO-GO for clinical operation.**
