# Independent fictional evaluation source notes

These sources inform broad warning categories. They do not clinically adjudicate the fictional cases or validate the implementation. All expectations remain AI-assisted provisional engineering checks requiring qualified clinical review. No source-specific medication or procedure advice is added by this evaluation.

Sources checked on 2026-10-26, before implementation access:

- [NHS heart attack](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heart-attack/): emergency symptom patterns include squeezing chest pain, radiation and severe breathing difficulty.
- [NHS shortness of breath](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/): severe breathing difficulty, inability to speak, colour change and child unresponsiveness are emergency warnings.
- [NHS anaphylaxis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/anaphylaxis/): suspected anaphylactic reactions involving self or another person need emergency attention.
- [American Heart Association heart attack/stroke symptoms](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms): sudden neurological warnings require emergency attention even if they resolve.
- [NHS seizures](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/what-to-do-if-someone-has-a-seizure-fit/): first/prolonged seizure and post-seizure breathing problems inform provisional warning coverage.
- [NHS emergency services](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/urgent-and-emergency-care-services/when-to-call-999/): broad life-threatening injury/illness categories.
- [NHS poisoning](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/poisoning/): suspected harmful ingestion, including medicines, requires immediate medical attention. No ingestion quantity is interpreted as safe here.
- [NHS headaches](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/headaches/): sudden extremely painful headache and neurological warning combinations are emergency categories.
- [NHS vomiting blood](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/vomiting-blood/): blood with faintness or dizziness is a serious warning pattern.

UK/US reference numbers are not local emergency-number configuration. Language scope is English only. Label adequacy, paediatric coverage, ambiguity thresholds, corrections and the boundary between history and recent events require qualified clinical review. A negative engineering label is not health clearance.
