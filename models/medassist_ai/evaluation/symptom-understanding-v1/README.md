# Fictional English understanding regression suite v1

The 115 utterances in [suite.json](suite.json) were manually specified with expected evidence/context/question outcomes before the new implementation. [suite.sha256](suite.sha256) binds those bytes. Expectations are not generated by the extractor or training data. They are AI-assisted engineering annotations, not clinical labels or human clinical approval. This set was used during development and must not be called independent held-out confirmation.

Cases cover 17 categories: tense/synonyms, spelling, negation, UNKNOWN, history/resolved, third-party, hypothetical, suspected diagnoses, ambiguous/unsupported details, multiple symptoms, contradiction/correction, time, qualifiers, typed question compatibility and explicit emergencies. The primary set exercises 18 evidence IDs. [Supplementary coverage](supplementary-coverage.json), written after primary development, adds canonical checks for the remaining mapped fields; its [results](supplementary-results.json) are separate.

Run `node --import tsx scripts/evaluate-symptom-understanding.ts <new-output.json>` from the repository root. The runner verifies the frozen suite hash, uses verified schema metadata, computes exact evidence precision/recall and per-case context/question/safety expectations, and refuses to overwrite receipts. It does not invoke a model/provider, access official records or generate gold labels.

[Initial](deterministic-initial.json), [refined](deterministic-refined.json) and [final](deterministic-final.json) outputs preserve the development history. The current `scripts/medassist-understanding.test.ts` also rechecks every frozen expectation after subsequent guards. **Two emergency expectations remain unmet** under the unchanged provisional rules: heart-attack and stroke declarations. Do not exclude them from safety reporting or change the expected outcomes to obtain a pass.

[Mock provider cases](mock-provider-cases.json) and [additional adversarial cases](additional-adversarial-cases.json) are manually authored JSON responses, including wrong actors/time/polarity, fabricated spans/IDs, unsafe extra fields, typed mismatch and cropped-negation examples. [Final mocked results](mocked-llm-final.json) test actual validation/context gates; they are not measurements of a real LLM. The [earlier mock receipt](mocked-llm.json) is retained.

No fresh live Groq calls were made for this suite. Live provider metrics from previous tasks must remain labelled historical. [Milestone findings, safety blockers and actual integration](../../docs/symptom-understanding.md) own the readiness interpretation.
