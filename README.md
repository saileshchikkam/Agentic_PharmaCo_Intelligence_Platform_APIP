# Agentic Pharmacovigilance Platform (APP)

> **An Intelligent AI Platform for Adverse Drug Reaction Reporting, Analysis, and Pharmacovigilance Decision Support.**

## 🚀 Overview

The **Agentic Pharmacovigilance Platform (APP)** is a research-driven, AI-powered web platform designed to improve the accessibility, quality, and efficiency of **Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reporting**.

The primary goal is not to showcase AI technologies, but to solve a real pharmacovigilance problem:

> **Make ADR reporting simple for patients and healthcare professionals while providing pharmacovigilance professionals with better-quality information and AI-assisted analysis.**

APP will progressively integrate:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Machine Learning
- Semantic Search
- Embeddings
- Explainable AI
- Multi-Agent AI
- Human-in-the-loop decision support

Technology will be introduced only when it directly or indirectly contributes to the project's primary objective.

---

## 🎯 Main Goal

To develop an intelligent pharmacovigilance platform that:

1. Makes ADR reporting easier and more accessible.
2. Helps users submit more complete and structured reports.
3. Improves the quality of information received by pharmacovigilance professionals.
4. Assists professionals in reviewing and analyzing ADR reports.
5. Provides evidence-grounded information through RAG.
6. Uses AI agents to automate appropriate multi-step pharmacovigilance workflows.
7. Keeps human professionals in control of important decisions.
