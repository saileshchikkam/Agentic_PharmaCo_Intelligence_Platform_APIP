# Day 001 — Project Initialization

**Project:** Agentic Pharmacovigilance Platform (APP)  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Research Foundation  
**Status:** 🟢 Started

---

## 🎯 Day 1 Objective

Establish the professional development workspace and finalize the core direction of the Agentic Pharmacovigilance Platform.

---

## ✅ Completed

### Project Vision

Defined APP as an AI-powered pharmacovigilance platform focused on improving:

- ADR reporting accessibility
- ADR report completeness
- ADR information quality
- Pharmacovigilance analysis
- Evidence retrieval
- Human decision support

### Core Philosophy

Established the project's primary principle:

> **Technology follows purpose.**

AI technologies will only be implemented when they contribute to solving the central ADR reporting and pharmacovigilance problem.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

Defined the primary users:

- Patients
- Doctors
- Nurses
- Pharmacists

Secondary users:

- Pharmacovigilance professionals
- Hospital pharmacovigilance units
- Researchers
- Pharmaceutical safety teams

---
