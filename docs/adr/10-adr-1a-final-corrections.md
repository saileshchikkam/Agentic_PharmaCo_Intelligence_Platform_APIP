# ADR-1A final corrections

2026-11-04. The guided fictional reporting flow remains English and text based.

The original PvPI Version 1.4 form and reporting instructions remain the first two pages. No `demo.pdf` was found in the workspace. The old exporter appended the complete confirmed case and unknown statuses on every export, and repeated overflow in that summary. Running the same fixtures through both versions measured five to two pages for the fitting case, with four suspected and three concomitant medicines, and six to three pages for the long reaction with one extra row of each kind.

The [widget inventory](templates/widget-inventory.json) covers all 147 original widgets. The generator keeps the explicit mappings for 113 text widgets and 19 checkboxes. It uses the age field for age when supplied and date of birth otherwise, applies DD/MM/YYYY to structured dates, and puts the previous report reference under Additional Information. It leaves the AMC/NCC, causality and signature areas empty. For multiline overflow, it retains the lines that fit plus a reference, then prints the remaining lines once. Narrow fields that cannot fit their value retain a reference to the full value. Extra medicine rows and unclassifiable confirmed action or reintroduction narratives have named continuation entries. Unknown-only extra rows produce no continuation.

The visible question and help text live in `src/features/adr/guided/questionCopy.ts`, separate from persisted keys and enum values. `GuidedWorkspace.tsx` and `guided.css` expand the workspace, keep medicine controls in relevant contexts and group the editable review. Enter advances a nonempty single-line answer. Ctrl+Enter or Cmd+Enter advances a narrative, and plain Enter inserts a newline. Back loads the saved answer. The existing request lock guards against duplicate saves. Unknown stays explicit, and edits can return to review.

Public Home → Log in or Sign up → authenticated Dashboard → MedAssist or guided ADR → Review → PvPI download. The route and history changes are in `AccessPreview.tsx`, `AccountSignIn.tsx`, and `navigation.ts`. The dashboard no longer requires a completed profile. Signed-out direct ADR access shows the sign-in gate and the separately labeled legacy preview. `ReportExport.tsx` describes the revised continuation rule.

Local frontend: <http://localhost:3000/#/>. Guided ADR: <http://localhost:3000/#/preview/adr-report>. The Express API shares port 3000. Browser and PDF evidence is generated by `scripts/adr-guided.browser.mjs` and `scripts/adr-pdf.test.py`. The latter writes the fitting and overflow samples under `output/pdf/` and page renders under `tmp/pdfs/`.

ADR-1B remains NO-GO for unconditional progression. The official form accepts one seriousness category and export asks the reporter to resolve multiple selections. The current form fonts support the English fixture path. The browser's built-in PDF support determines whether the embedded preview is visible, and the download remains available separately. Pharmacy and clinical validation remain separate.
