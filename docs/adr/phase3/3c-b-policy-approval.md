## Approved policy bundle v1

1. **Readiness — P01.** Sharing is a separate explicit action on an immutable saved revision. Apply the current deterministic required-field and chronology checks, show Unknowns and warnings, and require a revision-bound patient affirmation. Saving alone never submits or consents. No new clinical completeness claim.
2. **Recipient — P04.** Patient selects one currently approved, unexpired specialist who has explicitly opted into the prototype directory. No administrative routing, shared medical queue or automatic assignment. One active assignment per request; prevent simultaneous active requests for the same case until withdrawal.
3. **Directory disclosure — P04/P06/P13.** Authenticated directory exposes only opaque recipient reference, professional display name, approved category and organization. Clearly label these as prototype identity-approved, not independently verified real credentials. Exclude account email/phone, registration reference, private application fields, evidence, decisions and audit history. Eligibility and opt-in are rechecked server-side at read and submission.
4. **Disclosure — P05/P06.** Before consent, show the exact revision and recipient with an explicit list and preview of confirmed patient/reaction/medicine/reporter fields, explicit Unknowns and patient-authored statements. Share only confirmed information necessary for ADR review. Explicitly identify name, contact information and every other personal detail that the specialist will see in the original PvPI PDF, including continuation pages. If that exact revision’s original form contains information outside the approved disclosure, block sharing until the mismatch is resolved; never silently broaden consent or modify the original form to hide the mismatch. Exclude unconfirmed extraction proposals, internal account/profile data, other revisions, technical audit and reserved professional fields. Store the versioned disclosure projection/receipt; never expand it silently.
5. **Consent — P05/P07.** Unchecked affirmative checkbox and explicit final command authorize only the displayed projection of that saved revision for that recipient and internal APIP review. Prototype scope is synthetic self-reporting accounts; caregiver authority and real/minor data remain excluded. Consent wording/version and exact revision must be auditable. Neither specialist approval nor registration is patient consent.
6. **Revision changes — P07.** Future edits remain private. Sharing an updated revision or changing recipients requires a new disclosure and new recipient-specific consent. Do not retarget an existing grant in place.
7. **Withdrawal — P05/P07/P12.** Patient may withdraw an active request. After commit, subsequent protected reads are denied. Serialize sensitive state changes and recheck current consent/assignment/authorization immediately before returning identifiable content; a read already authorized and delivered cannot be recalled. Keep limited immutable consent/assignment/audit history. Withdrawn requests cannot be reopened by retrying old commands.
8. **Authorization/conflicts — P04/P11/P12/P13.** Each specialist read requires current approval and validity, active consent, active assignment and the exact granted revision. Deny self-review and disclosed conflicts; offer refusal/withdrawal without silently assigning someone else. Identity administrators get no clinical access, assignment powers or audit export merely from their role. Existing owner access remains independent.
9. **Inbox — P04/P06.** Assigned specialist sees only case reference, source revision, assignment date and internal request state, with a real owner-scoped count. No unassigned summaries or global draft search. Approved specialists without assignments see an actual empty state. Inactive specialists are denied.
10. **PDF — P14.** Permit an in-application preview of the original PvPI form generated from the exact consented immutable revision, including its instructions and necessary continuation pages. Each preview request and PDF delivery requires current unexpired specialist approval, active consent, active assignment and the exact revision grant; recheck immediately before delivering bytes. Disable the separate specialist PDF download action and do not provide a separate download endpoint. Before consent, clearly disclose that browser preview transfers PDF bytes, cannot guarantee prevention of copying, and previously delivered copies cannot be recalled. The preview must match the disclosed confirmed information; it grants no access to later revisions. Patient current/historical exports remain unchanged. This authorizes document preview only, not the 3C-C clinical review editor or notes.
11. **Retention — P08/P09.** Synthetic development records and minimal immutable access evidence are retained privately until a separately approved retention/disposal policy. No scheduled purge, permanent deletion or new DELETE right. Withdrawal removes access, not historical evidence. No real-data rollout under this interim prototype retention policy.
12. **Audit — P10/P15.** Atomically record consent, assignment, withdrawal, refusal and sensitive access decisions with verified actor, exact revision, recipient, command and policy version; never log narratives or credentials. Patients may see a bounded timeline for their own request. Specialists see their own assignment state, not global audit. Privileged audit export/incident handling and general operator access require separate approval; no public audit API.
13. **Completion boundary — P10/P16.** States are internal request/assignment states such as active, withdrawn and declined. Do not mark cases Reviewed, produce professional conclusions or imply government submission. Notes, clarification, final review and their visibility/retention rules remain blocked for 3C-C/D.
