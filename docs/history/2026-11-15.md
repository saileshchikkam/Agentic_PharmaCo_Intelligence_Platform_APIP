## MedAssist Phase B2: Symptom-Specific Precautions and Healthcare Specialty Intelligence

### What Changed
- **Symptom-Specific Precautions Knowledge Base**: Implemented structured, evidence-backed knowledge base covering all 11 core symptom categories (`respiratory`, `fever_general`, `headache_neurological`, `digestive_abdominal`, `skin_dermatological`, `ent`, `eye_ophthalmological`, `musculoskeletal`, `dental_oral`, `urinary_renal`, `pediatric_pregnancy`) in Python (`models/medassist_ai/precautions/precautions_kb.json`) and TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-precautions/precautions-data.ts`).
  - Each record incorporates symptom triggers, applicability conditions, exclusion criteria, safe actionable comfort steps (e.g. hydration, steam, positioning), explicit red-flag warning signs, what to avoid (e.g. unprescribed antibiotics, aspirin in children, vigorous exertion), authoritative references (NICE Clinical Knowledge Summaries, NHS Health A-Z, WHO IMCI, CDC, American Academy guidelines), provisional clinical status (`provisional_research_rule_unreviewed`), and content versioning.
  - Strictly no medication prescriptions, drug dosages, or definitive clinical diagnoses.
- **Urgency-Based Safe Guidance**: Built tailored guidance engine across all urgency tiers:
  - *Low*: Self-care, non-pharmacological comfort steps, symptom monitoring, hydration, and warning sign re-evaluation.
  - *Medium*: Timely outpatient GP or specialist consultation within 24–48 hours, what information to prepare for the doctor, symptoms to track.
  - *High*: Same-day urgent assessment (within 2–4 hours), safe precautions awaiting transport, what NOT to do (e.g. no driving), companion requirements.
  - *Extreme*: Immediate emergency services notification (112/108/ambulance), critical first-aid steps while waiting (W-position, airway, calm), what NOT to do, and critical timeline/medication information for paramedics.
  - *Unable to assess*: Explicit statements of uncertainty, fallback in-person medical evaluation advice.
- **Healthcare Specialty Intelligence**: Deterministic mapping across all 12 medical specialties (`general_medicine`, `family_medicine`, `pulmonology`, `ent`, `dermatology`, `ophthalmology`, `orthopaedics`, `gastroenterology`, `dentistry`, `paediatrics`, `obstetrics_gynaecology`, `emergency_medicine`).
  - Provides primary specialty + alternative specialty for symptom overlaps, comprehensive clinical rationale, age-based routing (<18 to Paediatrics), and absolute emergency override to Emergency Medicine when urgent/emergency signs are detected.
- **Phase B3 Facility Navigation Contract**: Defined downstream contract `medassist.facility-navigation.v1` supporting all 10 healthcare facility tiers without commercial brand or size bias:
  1. Clinics & Polyclinics
  2. Primary Health Centres (PHC)
  3. Community Health Centres (CHC)
  4. Small Private Hospitals & Nursing Homes
  5. Government General / District Hospitals
  6. Private Multi-Specialty Hospitals
  7. Super-Specialty Hospitals & Tertiary Care
  8. Medical College Hospitals & Teaching Institutions
  9. Diagnostic Centres & Imaging Facilities
  10. 24x7 Emergency Departments & Trauma Care
- **Conversational UI & Evidence Export**: Integrated specialty recommendations, facility tier capabilities, and symptom-specific comfort precautions into `src/features/medassist-conversation/MedAssistConversation.tsx` assessment status drawer and exported session JSON.
- **Model A Stage 6 Isolation**: Confirmed Model A background worker (PID 14752) remained completely undisturbed and continuously active during all development.

### Why
- Bridge the clinical gap between conversational symptom gathering, urgency assessment, and actionable next steps while strictly avoiding unauthorized diagnostic claims or pharmacological prescribing.
- Provide objective, unbiased healthcare specialty intelligence and prepare facility navigation capabilities for Phase B3 Google Maps integration.
