## MedAssist Phase B2: Symptom-Specific Precautions and Healthcare Specialty Intelligence

### What Changed
- **Symptom-Specific Precautions Knowledge Base**: Implemented structured, evidence-backed knowledge base covering all 11 core symptom categories (`respiratory`, `fever_general`, `headache_neurological`, `digestive_abdominal`, `skin_dermatological`, `ent`, `eye_ophthalmological`, `musculoskeletal`, `dental_oral`, `urinary_renal`, `pediatric_pregnancy`) in Python (`models/medassist_ai/precautions/precautions_kb.json`) and TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-precautions/precautions-data.ts`).
  - Each record incorporates symptom triggers, applicability conditions, exclusion criteria, safe actionable comfort steps (e.g. hydration, steam, positioning), explicit red-flag warning signs, what to avoid (e.g. unprescribed antibiotics, aspirin in children, vigorous exertion), authoritative references (NICE Clinical Knowledge Summaries, NHS Health A-Z, WHO IMCI, CDC, American Academy guidelines), provisional clinical status (`provisional_research_rule_unreviewed`), and content versioning.
  - Strictly no medication prescriptions, drug dosages, or definitive clinical diagnoses.
- **Urgency-Based Safe Guidance**: Built tailored guidance engine across all urgency tiers:
  - *Low*: Self-care, non-pharmacological comfort steps, symptom monitoring, hydration, and warning sign re-evaluation.
  - *Medium*: Timely outpatient GP or specialist consultation within 24–48 hours, what information to prepare for the doctor, symptoms to track.
  - *High*: Same-day urgent assessment (within 2–4 hours), safe precautions awaiting transport, what NOT to do (e.g. no driving), companion requirements.
  - *Extreme*: Immediate emergency services notification (112/108/ambulance), critical first-aid steps while waiting (W-position, airway, calm), what NOT to do, and critical timeline/medication information for paramedics.
  - *Unable to assess*: Explicit statements of uncertainty, fallback in-person medical evaluation advice.
- **Healthcare Specialty Intelligence**: Deterministic mapping across all 12 medical specialties (`general_medicine`, `family_medicine`, `pulmonology`, `ent`, `dermatology`, `ophthalmology`, `orthopaedics`, `gastroenterology`, `dentistry`, `paediatrics`, `obstetrics_gynaecology`, `emergency_medicine`).
  - Provides primary specialty + alternative specialty for symptom overlaps, comprehensive clinical rationale, age-based routing (<18 to Paediatrics), and absolute emergency override to Emergency Medicine when urgent/emergency signs are detected.
- **Phase B3 Facility Navigation Contract**: Defined downstream contract `medassist.facility-navigation.v1` supporting all 10 healthcare facility tiers without commercial brand or size bias:
  1. Clinics & Polyclinics
  2. Primary Health Centres (PHC)
  3. Community Health Centres (CHC)
  4. Small Private Hospitals & Nursing Homes
  5. Government General / District Hospitals
  6. Private Multi-Specialty Hospitals
  7. Super-Specialty Hospitals & Tertiary Care
  8. Medical College Hospitals & Teaching Institutions
  9. Diagnostic Centres & Imaging Facilities
  10. 24x7 Emergency Departments & Trauma Care
- **Conversational UI & Evidence Export**: Integrated specialty recommendations, facility tier capabilities, and symptom-specific comfort precautions into `src/features/medassist-conversation/MedAssistConversation.tsx` assessment status drawer and exported session JSON.
- **Model A Stage 6 Isolation**: Confirmed Model A background worker (PID 14752) remained completely undisturbed and continuously active during all development.

## MedAssist Phase B3: Symptom-Matched Healthcare Facility Discovery and Google Maps Integration

### What Changed
- **Versioned Facility Discovery Contracts**: Defined versioned contracts `medassist.facility-discovery-request.v1` and `medassist.facility-discovery.v1` in TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-discovery/discovery-contracts.ts`) and Python (`models/medassist_ai/hospital/contracts.py`), strictly consuming `medassist.facility-navigation.v1` and enforcing complete patient privacy (zero PII, symptoms, or transcripts transmitted).
- **5-Priority Intelligent Facility Ranking Engine**: Implemented deterministic matching and ranking across all 10 facility categories in Python (`models/medassist_ai/hospital/discovery_engine.py`) and TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-discovery/discovery-engine.ts`):
  - Priority 1: Required clinical capabilities (exact specialty or alternative match; emergency resuscitation if emergency).
  - Priority 2: Appropriate urgency and known availability (currently open or 24x7).
  - Priority 3: Estimated driving duration.
  - Priority 4: Road distance.
  - Priority 5: Tier appropriateness without commercial or size bias.
  - Core Accessibility Rule strictly enforced: a nearby single-doctor clinic (`FAC_CLINIC_01` at 1.2 km, 6 mins) ranks ahead of distant large hospitals (`FAC_PVT_MULTI_01` at 6.2 km, `FAC_SUPER_01` at 14.5 km) for routine/outpatient urgency.
- **Wrong-Specialty & Emergency Capability Filtering**:
  - Unrelated facilities (e.g. dental or eye clinics for respiratory complaints) are strictly rejected.
  - Diagnostic centres are restricted and never substituted for medical consultation or emergency treatment.
  - In emergencies, outpatient clinics lacking 24x7 emergency resuscitation are excluded; dedicated emergency departments and trauma centers are prioritized with prominent 112/108 call buttons.
- **Google Maps Platform Integration & Safe Fallback**:
  - Implemented `server/medassist-maps-provider.ts` and `models/medassist_ai/hospital/maps_provider.py` with Places API (New) Text Search (`places:searchText`) and Routes API (`computeRoutes`).
  - Added server-side endpoint `POST /api/research/medassist/facilities` with strict request validation and trusted local request protection.
  - When API key is absent, empty, or calls fail, safely falls back to deterministic multi-tier reference fixtures (`models/medassist_ai/hospital/facility_fixtures.json`, `src/features/medassist-discovery/discovery-fixtures.ts`) with explicit status `simulated_fixtures`. Live results are never fabricated.
- **Privacy & Location Security**:
  - Explicit device location consent with error handling (permission denied fallback to manual locality).
  - Only approximate coordinates (~100m) or manual locality string are used; zero patient names, symptoms, or chat histories are ever sent to external providers.
- **Conversational UI & Facility Cards**:
  - Upgraded `src/features/medassist-safety/HospitalNavigation.tsx` into an interactive discovery panel displaying provider status banner, filter toggle (verified registry only vs all), facility cards with distance, drive time, hours, address, match reasons, and action links ("Open in Maps", "Get Directions", "Call").
  - Linked "Find nearby facilities" button in the Assessment drawer of `MedAssistConversation.tsx`.

### Why
- Complete the end-to-end patient assistance pathway by connecting assessed clinical urgency and recommended specialty to appropriate, accessible physical healthcare facilities without commercial bias or hospital size preference.
