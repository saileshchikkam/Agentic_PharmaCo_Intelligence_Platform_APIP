# APIP Development History — 2026-11-15

## MedAssist Phase B1: Patient-Reported Severity and Hybrid Urgency Assessment

### What Changed
- **Patient-Reported Severity**: Added natural conversational discomfort question (*"How uncomfortable are your symptoms right now?"*) with 5 options (*Low, Medium, High, Extreme, Not sure*) within the 8–10 question interview target.
- **Natural Understanding Layer**: Implemented deterministic NLP parser `src/features/medassist-severity/severity-understanding.ts` supporting typo tolerance, colloquial Indian English descriptors (*"thoda"*, *"bahut zyada"*, *"too much paining"*), negations (*"not extreme, just mild"*), corrections, and symptom-specific severity breakdowns.
- **Hybrid Urgency Engine**: Created rule-based research engine in TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-severity/urgency-engine.ts`) and Python (`models/medassist_ai/severity/urgency_engine.py`) with documented rule metadata (`models/medassist_ai/severity/rules.json`). The engine separates self-reported severity from evaluated urgency.
- **Safety Precedence**: Enforced emergency warning sign overrides (respiratory distress, ambulance calls); a patient reporting "Low" discomfort never downgrades an identified emergency. Fail-closed behavior on missing evidence or contradictions.
- **Demographic Vulnerability Floor**: Enforced pediatric fever (< 2 years) and geriatric systemic illness (>= 65 years with fever and pain/dizziness) minimum High urgency floors.
- **Downstream Contracts**: Formally defined versioned contract `medassist.urgency-assessment.v1` in both TypeScript (`src/features/medassist-severity/severity-contracts.ts`) and Python (`models/medassist_ai/severity/contracts.py`).
- **Conversational UI & Export**: Integrated severity question and live reactive assessment status drawer into `src/features/medassist-conversation/MedAssistConversation.tsx` and session JSON download.
- **Model A Stage 6 Background Worker**: Launched `scripts/run_model_a_stage6.py` under PID 14752 (`task-2218`) and registered 30-minute unattended monitoring cron (`task-2224`), preserving locked test isolation and existing Stage 1–5 artifacts.

### Why
- Establish the complete research-only severity and clinical urgency safety foundation for MedAssist without requiring unapproved ML triage models or new Model B training datasets.
- Ensure emergency safety rules always take precedence over user subjective discomfort ratings while maintaining conversational flexibility.
