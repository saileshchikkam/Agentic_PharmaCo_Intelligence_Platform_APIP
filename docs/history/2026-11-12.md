## MedAssist — natural symptom understanding improvements, v2 evaluation suite, and Stage 6 recovery runner

Completed comprehensive hardening and evaluation of the MedAssist natural language symptom understanding layer and added Stage 6 recovery support on branch `feat/medassist-jayanth`:

1. **Deterministic Concept Expansion & Polarity Scoping (`src/features/medassist-conversation/`):**
   - In `concepts.ts`, added allowlisted whole-token phone misspellings, colloquial Indian English descriptors, and symptom variants across `E_53` (`"hed paining"`, `"hedache"`, `"hed ache"`, `"aching hed"`, `"belly ache bad"`, `"stomach pain coming going"`), `E_201` (`"coffin"`), `E_91` (`"body burning hot"`, `"burning hot body"`), `E_97` (`"soar throat"`, `"sore thrt"`, `"throat feels soor"`), `E_148` (`"nausia"`, `"pukey feeling"`, `"feel like pukin"`), `E_211` (`"vomting repeatedly"`), `E_51` (`"motions loose"`, `"motions loose from morning"`), `E_94` (`"chils"`, `"chillls"`), `E_89` (`"always tierd"`, `"tierd"`), and `E_76` (`"dizzzy"`, `"feel dizzzy"`, `"head spinning"`).
   - In `concepts.ts` and `understanding.ts`, prioritized `E_144` (diffuse muscle pain) over general pain `E_53` when `"body is aching all over"` is stated, preventing duplicate generic pain claims.
   - In `language-context.ts`, implemented concept-bounded sub-clause lookahead splitting across contrastive boundaries (`without`, `except`, `excluding`, `instead of`, `rather than`, `only <symptom>`, `not <symptom>`, `now <symptom>`, `<symptom> yes|no`). Only splits when preceding text contains a supported concept, preserving uncertainty prefixes (`"i think i have fever"` remains a single clause).
   - In `language-context.ts`, expanded `uncertaintyCue` with typo variants (`don not know`, `dont know`, `do nt know`, `dunno`, `wonder(?:ing)? if|whether`, `doubt(?:ing)? if|whether`) and generalized third-person subject attribution to detect bare relationship/colleague nouns (`"coworker"`, `"friend"`, `"relative"`).
   - In `pain-scope.ts` and `natural.ts`, added contrastive pain exception handling (`no headache but stomach pain`), allowing positive pain confirmation without triggering `SOURCE_CONFLICT` rejects.
   - In `understanding.ts`, expanded intra-utterance retraction markers (`scratch that`, `take that back`, `wait actually no`, `wait no`) to preserve self-contradictions cleanly as `state: 'contradiction'`, prompting MedAssist to clarify with the user rather than guessing.
