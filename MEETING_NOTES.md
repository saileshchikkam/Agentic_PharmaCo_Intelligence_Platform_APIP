# Meeting 001 — Project Foundation

**Day:** 1

## Main Discussion

The project direction was refined from a broad "AI-powered ADR system" into a focused pharmacovigilance platform.

---

## Core Problem Identified

People may experience adverse reactions after taking medicines but may:

- Not know that ADR reporting exists.
- Not know where to report.
- Find traditional reporting workflows difficult.
- Submit incomplete information.

Pharmacovigilance professionals need useful, structured information for subsequent analysis.

---

## Core Solution

APP will focus on:

> Making ADR reporting easier for users and making submitted reports more useful for pharmacovigilance professionals.

---
